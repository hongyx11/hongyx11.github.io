My group works on high-performance computing, parallel matrix computation, and sparse matrix optimization. I am interested in students who enjoy understanding both algorithms and computer systems.

## Preparation I look for

- Working knowledge of C or C++.
- Comfort with the Linux terminal.
- Foundations in computer architecture and numerical linear algebra.
- Patience with measurement, debugging, and careful experimental work.

You do not need to know every parallel programming model before contacting me. The [HPC Starter](/hpc-starter/) series provides a practical route through the expected foundations.

## What I offer

- Access to modern supercomputing resources.
- Mentoring on research questions and experimental design.
- Help getting started with HPC software and research workflows.
- Projects that connect algorithms, applications, and architecture.

## Before you email

1. Read the [Research](/research/) page and identify a direction that interests you.
2. Complete at least the first three HPC Starter lessons.
3. Look through [IU Sparse](https://github.com/hongyx11/iusparse) or another project and note one question you have.
4. Send a concise email to [yuxihong@iu.edu](mailto:yuxihong@iu.edu) with your CV, background, interests, and the question you would like to explore.
