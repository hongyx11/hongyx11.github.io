<p class="eyebrow">HIGH-PERFORMANCE COMPUTING · SPARSE ALGORITHMS</p>

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Intelligent Systems Engineering at [Indiana University Bloomington](https://luddy.iu.edu/people/hong-yuxi.html). I design scalable parallel matrix algorithms for scientific computing and machine-learning workloads.

My work exploits sparsity and structure in scientific data to reduce computation and communication, bringing large problems within reach of modern CPUs, GPUs, and emerging accelerators. I have published more than 20 peer-reviewed papers in venues including SC, ISC, and IJHPCA. Our work on algebraic compression for seismic processing was named a **2023 Gordon Bell Prize finalist**.

## Short biography

Yuxi Hong is an Assistant Professor of Intelligent Systems Engineering at Indiana University Bloomington. He earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science from King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST), and his M.S. in Electronics Engineering and B.S. from Tsinghua University. His research interests include high-performance computing, numerical linear algebra, GPU programming, sparse computation, low-rank methods, and efficient machine learning. His Ph.D. advisors were David Keyes, Hatem Ltaief, and Matteo Ravasi.
